# Brag Plan: Medical Risk XAI

## What is this app?
Medical Risk XAI is a multi-tenant clinical cardiovascular risk assessment platform that combines machine learning (XGBoost, Random Forest, Logistic Regression) with SHAP explainability across 21 clinical parameters, giving doctors auditable risk scores with zero cross-hospital data leakage.

## The angle
Cardiovascular risk predictions shouldn't be black boxes. Medical Risk XAI gives doctors and hospital networks auditable, transparent machine learning with real-time SHAP feature attribution—benchmarked on 15+ curated datasets.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Big cinematic typography: "PREDICT RISK WITH TRANSPARENT, EXPLAINABLE AI" with a pulsing glowing teal & lime pulse ring.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Live Clinical Assessment Engine**: Real-time evaluation of patient parameters (Age, Blood Pressure, Cholesterol, Fasting Blood Sugar) feeding directly into the XGBoost model.
- **SHAP Feature Attribution**: Instant visual breakdown of top risk drivers (+32% Systolic BP, +24% Serum Cholesterol, +18% Age).
- **Multi-Tenant Scale & Benchmark**: 15+ Curated Datasets · 21 Clinical Features · 3 Benchmarked Models · 100% Explainable.

## Outro / punchline
"Every score explainable. Every hospital secure." Logo slam with glowing cross emblem and "Medical Risk XAI".

## User flow worth showing
Patient Clinical Data Entry (Age 58, BP 142/90, Chol 235 mg/dL) → XGBoost Engine Evaluation & SHAP attribution breakdown → Real-Time Hospital Risk Dashboard & Multi-Tenant Isolation confirmed.
